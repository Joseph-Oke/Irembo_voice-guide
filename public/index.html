Irembo Voice Guide helps people in rural Rwanda apply for a birth certificate on Irembo without having to read long instructions or pay someone to do it for them. It walks the user through the process one step at a time with a short voice note and text, saves their place, and lets them tap Done, Repeat or I'm stuck. If they are stuck twice, it sends the contact of a verified Irembo agent. It does not replace Irembo: users still complete each step on the Irembo website or app, and the guide only shows them the way. Administrators keep the steps and the agent list up to date and can see how many people start and finish.
